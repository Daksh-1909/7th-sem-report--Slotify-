\chapter{Conclusion}

\section{Summary of Work Done}
The Slotify project was developed to solve the recurring scheduling and coordination issues faced by service-based businesses. The platform brings together appointment booking, staff management, business scheduling, customer communication, and review handling into a unified system. By addressing the common problems of missed appointments, manual coordination, and scheduling conflicts, the project demonstrates a practical solution for modern service businesses.

\section{Objectives Met}
The project successfully met its major objectives by enabling customers to search for services and book appointments online, helping businesses manage staff and working schedules, and supporting automated reminders to improve booking reliability. It also introduced role-based access and a structured application flow that makes the system suitable for real operational use.

\section{Challenges Encountered}
Several technical and practical challenges were encountered during development, especially in the areas of slot validation, concurrency handling, and user-role management. Synchronizing booking data across multiple users while maintaining consistency was one of the most significant technical challenges. In addition, the system had to balance usability and complexity so that it remained understandable for business users while supporting backend reliability.

\section{Key Takeaways}
The project highlighted the importance of designing scheduling systems around real business problems rather than generic software features. A strong appointment system must balance customer convenience, business efficiency, and technical reliability. Slotify demonstrates that this balance is achievable through careful design, modular development, and strong validation of booking logic.
