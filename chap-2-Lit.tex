\chapter{Literature Survey}

\section{Overview}
Appointment scheduling plays a central role in service-based businesses such as salons, clinics, beauty centers, repair shops, and wellness studios. In such businesses, each booking affects staff workload, customer satisfaction, and revenue generation. A poor scheduling system can create double bookings, missed appointments, idle time, and unnecessary manual work. As a result, scheduling is not only a convenience feature but also an operational need.

Many small businesses still rely on phone calls, manual calendars, or informal record keeping. Although this approach may work at a very small scale, it becomes difficult to manage as business size and customer demand increase. Literature in scheduling and operations management highlights that effective booking systems should reduce confusion, improve utilization, and support timely communication between customers and service providers.

\section{Paper Reviews}
A review of scheduling and service management literature indicates that appointment systems are most successful when they support real-time coordination, reduce administrative overhead, and improve customer communication. Earlier systems were mainly designed to automate calendars, but later solutions expanded to include reminders, confirmations, and availability checks.

Research in service operations suggests that booking systems are most effective when they help businesses manage time slots without creating scheduling conflicts. Frequent issues in conventional systems include conflict between staff schedules, poor visibility of occupancy, and limited support for service-specific duration and availability. These problems become more severe in businesses where different treatments require different durations and different staff skills.

In addition, the literature on customer behavior and service delivery emphasizes that missed appointments and late confirmations create financial losses and reduce customer trust. This is particularly relevant for service-based industries where customer retention depends on convenience, reliability, and professional communication. Therefore, the design of an effective booking system must consider both operational efficiency and customer experience.

Several studies in digital service technology also highlight that automation improves coordination when it is integrated into the booking cycle rather than used as an isolated add-on. For example, reminder messages, appointment confirmation, and real-time availability checks improve the efficiency of scheduling by reducing no-shows and helping customers take timely action. This is one of the major motivations behind developing a dedicated platform such as Slotify.

\section{Reminder Effectiveness}
Reminder systems are widely recognized as one of the most effective ways to reduce missed appointments and improve scheduling reliability. When a customer receives a confirmation and reminder before the appointment time, the chance of no-show decreases significantly. This effect is important in service businesses because even a small number of unfilled slots can cause lost revenue and poor customer flow.

The effectiveness of reminders is not only due to memory support but also because they create a stronger communication loop between the customer and the business. Automated reminders reduce reliance on manual follow-ups and ensure that customers are informed about their booking status. They also help businesses recover last-minute cancellations by allowing the time slot to be offered to other customers more quickly.

In this context, reminders are not an optional feature but a core part of the booking experience. A scheduling system that includes reminders can deliver better punctuality, lower cancellation risk, and stronger service continuity. This is one reason why Slotify emphasizes confirmation and reminder functionality as part of its core design.

\section{Research Gap / What the Review Did Not Resolve}
Although existing scheduling platforms offer useful features, they still do not completely solve the challenges faced by local service businesses. Many solutions are generic, while the actual needs of salons, clinics, therapy centers, and repair shops are often highly specific. The review also shows that no single platform fully combines flexible business management, multi-staff coordination, low-cost deployment, and customer-focused scheduling in a simplified form.

Another gap is the limited focus on service-specific business workflows. Many systems support appointment creation but do not adequately support staff availability rules, split scheduling, operational analytics, or business customization. In many cases, the solution is designed from the software side rather than from the actual daily challenges faced by business owners.

This review therefore highlights the need for a dedicated platform that is practical, scalable, and tailored to real service operations. Slotify is intended to fill this gap by combining online appointment booking, real-time availability checks, reminder communication, and business-oriented scheduling control in one unified system.
