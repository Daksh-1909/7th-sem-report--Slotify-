10 & Agarwal \& Varghese (2024)\cite{agarwal2024}  & AI finance assistant with budgeting and alerts & Privacy, trust, and demographic issues \\ \hline

12 & Schroeder et al. (2020)\cite{schroeder2020}  & ML architecture for robo-advisory in stock and crypto markets & No expense tracking or personal budgeting \\ \hline

16 & Shailendra et al. (2025)\cite{shailendra2025}  & ML platform with multilingual support and data encryption & No real-time data or context-aware advisory \\ \hline

17 & Shaik et al. (2025)\cite{shaik2025}  & ML-based automatic expense categorization system & Gap in real-time adoption in mainstream apps \\ \hline

18 & Suganya et al. (2025)\cite{suganya2025} & GenAI finance consultant using Llama 3.1 + Random Forest + GAN & Needs real-world validation across demographics \\ \hline

19 & Ponnuviji et al. (2025)\cite{ponnuviji2025}  & Review of ML, NLP, predictive analytics in finance apps & No unified budgeting + investment + literacy platform \\ \hline

20 & Pangavhane et al. (2023)\cite{pangavhane2023}  & AI finance advisor with risk assessment and portfolio optimization & Lacks real-time advisory for combined finance tasks \\ \hline

Based on the literature review, it is evident that while many systems address expense tracking and prediction, there is a clear gap in providing integrated intelligent advisory, real-time over-limit alerts, spending behavior classification, and personalized recommendations within a single platform. The proposed FinanceAI system is specifically designed to address all these gaps.
