\chapter{Software Requirements Specification (SRS)}

\section{Purpose and Scope}
The primary purpose of Slotify is to securely provide a comprehensive, fully digital appointment booking system explicitly designed for service-based businesses. These businesses urgently need a highly reliable, always-on way to accurately manage incoming appointments, intelligently balance staff schedules across different shifts, and maintain continuous, professional customer communication. The system is fundamentally designed from the ground up to support 24/7 online booking without requiring human intervention, seamlessly accommodate impromptu walk-in scheduling at the front desk, dispatch fully automated, branded confirmation and reminder notifications via email, and supply business owners with high-level, actionable analytics. By centralizing all of these disparate operations into a single cohesive platform, the system drastically reduces the need for constant manual coordination, heavily mitigates the persistent risk of human-induced scheduling errors, and significantly elevates the overall professional image and efficiency of the business.

The extensive scope of the project purposefully includes a robust, mobile-responsive customer-facing booking interface, highly granular staff management tools, a powerful, data-rich administrative dashboard designed for comprehensive business oversight, intelligent automated notifications driven by booking state changes, and exceptionally strict real-time slot validation algorithms to absolutely prevent overlaps. The application is entirely focused on fulfilling the highly specific, fast-paced needs of service-oriented businesses such as hair salons, specialized medical clinics, physical therapy centers, and automotive repair shops. In these highly dynamic and often unpredictable environments, precise appointment timing, accurate service duration tracking, and dynamic staff reallocation are absolutely essential to maintaining smooth daily operations, minimizing waiting room congestion, and ultimately maximizing daily revenue potential.

\section{User Roles}
The system supports multiple user roles with distinct responsibilities and access rights.

\subsection{Customer}
Customers are the end-users who interact with the frontend of the platform to schedule their visits. They can intuitively search for specific businesses by name or category, view clearly listed available services and their respective durations, select convenient time slots based on real-time availability, and securely book appointments online from their personal devices. Following a booking, they also automatically receive structured email confirmations and timely reminders to ensure they do not miss their slot, and they may be prompted to provide valuable feedback after a completed service session.

\subsection{Business Admin}
Business administrators are typically the business owners or senior managers who require full control over the operational parameters of the establishment. They possess elevated privileges to meticulously manage the business's core working hours, configure service offerings and their associated pricing, onboard and manage detailed staff profiles, and enforce global business policies (such as cancellation rules). Furthermore, they can holistically monitor all incoming and historical bookings, actively manage staff schedules to prevent burnout, and review aggregated business performance metrics through an interactive dashboard.

\subsection{Staff Member}
Staff members are the actual service providers, such as stylists, doctors, or technicians, who execute the booked services. They can securely log into their individual portals to view their daily or weekly assigned appointments, confirm their attendance, block out specific times for breaks, and officially mark service sessions as completed. They play a crucial role in maintaining an accurate, up-to-date schedule for the business by dynamically updating their availability status, which directly influences what time slots are shown to customers.

\subsection{Reception / Walk-In User}
The receptionist or front desk user operates as the operational bridge between the physical storefront and the digital scheduling system. This role is specifically designed to create manual, walk-in bookings for customers who arrive without prior notice or for those who prefer to book their next appointment in person before leaving. This crucial role ensures that the digital calendar remains a true reflection of the physical occupancy of the business, preventing conflicts between online bookers and walk-in clients.

\section{Functional Requirements}
Slotify must provide functional capabilities that support appointment booking, schedule management, communication, and reporting.

\begin{itemize}
\item The system must intuitively allow a customer to browse a registered business profile, view its specific details, and seamlessly select a desired service from a categorized list.
\item The system must dynamically calculate and display only strictly valid time slots based on a complex intersection of the business's operating hours, the specific selected staff member's availability, the duration of the chosen service, and any pre-existing bookings.
\item The system must strictly prevent double booking at all costs through robust server-side validation and atomic database-level constraints, even when multiple users attempt to book the exact same slot concurrently.
\item The system must automatically generate and dispatch richly formatted confirmation emails immediately upon successful booking, and trigger scheduled reminder notifications a predetermined time before the appointment to minimize no-shows.
\item The system must explicitly support the rapid creation of walk-in appointments directly from the admin or receptionist panel to accommodate physical customers without requiring them to create a digital account.
\item The system must securely allow authenticated staff and admins to view, edit, reschedule, or cancel bookings, as well as seamlessly track status changes (e.g., pending, completed, cancelled) throughout the appointment lifecycle.
\item The system must optionally support a structured review submission process after a completed appointment, allowing businesses to gather critical customer feedback and continuously improve their service quality.
\end{itemize}

\section{Non-Functional Requirements}
The Slotify application must be architecturally designed to be structurally reliable, highly performant under load, rigorously secure against modern web threats, and engineered in a way that makes it easy for future development teams to maintain and scale over an extended lifecycle. Performance is an absolutely critical metric, especially during known peak booking periods (such as weekends, holiday seasons, or immediately following promotional campaigns) when multiple customers may concurrently query the database for availability or attempt to furiously reserve the exact same highly sought-after time slot. The backend system must process these concurrent requests with minimal possible latency to provide a snappy, frictionless user experience. Security must be strictly and consistently enforced at every single layer of the stack through robust token-based authentication (JWT), explicit and heavily tested role-based access control (RBAC) middleware, and strict multi-tenant data isolation logic to ensure that one business can never accidentally or maliciously access, modify, or delete another business's proprietary client data. Maintainability also matters immensely because the system is explicitly designed to be iteratively extended with complex future features such as native third-party payment integrations, advanced AI-driven predictive analytics, and dedicated native mobile application support. Therefore, the entire codebase must remain exceptionally clean, thoroughly documented, and highly modular to support this future growth without requiring a total rewrite.

\section{Constraints}
The Slotify system is inevitably constrained by several stringent real-world operational requirements and technical limitations. Real-world business schedules vary wildly by the day of the week, by the specific staff member, and by the intricate category of the service being offered. The software solution must therefore natively support incredibly flexible working hours, including split shifts, variable break times, and dynamic service-specific durations that can override defaults. The system also fundamentally needs to operate flawlessly under a complex multi-business, multi-tenant cloud environment, where absolute data isolation, strict schema separation, and rigorously secure, role-based access are not merely preferred, but legally and ethically essential. In addition to these domain constraints, the project is naturally limited by highly practical implementation constraints such as fixed development time, limited initial server deployment environments, and the overarching, non-negotiable need to keep the entire system interface simple enough for non-technical business users to adopt seamlessly without requiring an extensive IT manual or dedicated technical overhead.
